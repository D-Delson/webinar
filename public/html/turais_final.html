Common questions

New to app development? That's okay.

Here are a few things most first-time founders want to know before contacting us.

Do I need to know coding to talk to Turais?

No. The service is designed for people who understand their business idea but need help with technical planning and developer communication.

Is the first call really free?

Yes. The initial 15-minute discovery call is free. We use it to understand your project and explain what kind of help may be relevant. Detailed reviews, documents and ongoing support are separate paid services.

Will Turais develop my app?

No. Turais Clarity is an independent advisory service. The actual software is developed by the developer or agency you choose.

I already hired a developer. Can you still help?

Yes. We can discuss your current project, including delays, unclear scope or difficulty reviewing progress. Any detailed assessment requires a separately agreed scope.

How much do your paid services cost?

That depends on the size of your app, its current stage and the help required. We'll explain the work and share a quotation before you commit.

What about privacy and my app idea?

You can describe your idea at a high level in the enquiry. If we need confidential documents or sensitive project details, we can discuss appropriate confidentiality terms before sharing them.

Can you guarantee a launch date or fixed development budget?

No independent advisor can guarantee how a third-party development team will perform. Our role is to help make scope, expectations and progress easier to review so you can make better decisions.
